Engineer.

I studied Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan, with a minor in Mathematics. I like understanding the whole system: the signal coming in, the code making a decision, and the hardware doing something with it.

I’m especially interested in technologies that help people interact with the world, including brain–computer interfaces, prosthetics, and medical technology. That curiosity also takes me into processors, embedded systems, and machine learning.

This is a home for what I’m building, what I’m learning, and a little of life outside engineering.
