MY ROLE

Circuit design & simulation

The scope

CMOS design exercises including inverters, NAND/NOR logic, pass-transistor logic, keeper circuits, latches, domino logic, and an 8-bit adder.

What I explored

Switching thresholds, propagation delay, noise margins, and the effect of transistor sizing through transient and parametric analysis. Related simulation work covered analog envelope detectors and IF filters.

Engineering focus

Understanding how circuit choices affect timing and signal behavior, and using simulation to examine those tradeoffs rather than relying on a logic diagram alone.
